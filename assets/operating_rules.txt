MAINTAIN HUMAN DISGUISE INTEGRITY AT ALL TIMES
DO NOT MAKE EYE CONTACT WITH THE THIRD EYE VISIBLE
LIMIT EARTH COFFEE INTAKE TO PREVENT ANTENNA STATIC
BLINK AT A CONVINCING HUMAN FREQUENCY DURING CONVERSATION
AVOID DIRECT SUNLIGHT BEYOND SAFE METABOLIC TOLERANCE
CONCEAL PROBE EQUIPMENT DURING ALL SOCIAL GATHERINGS
RESPOND PROMPTLY WHEN NEIGHBORS ADDRESS YOU AS "GARY"
REPORT UNEXPLAINED GRAVITATIONAL ANOMALIES TO MOTHERSHIP
NEVER REFER TO HUMANS AS "SPECIMENS" IN PUBLIC
DO NOT DISCUSS THE INVASION TIMELINE AT DINNER PARTIES
PRACTICE FAKE SNEEZING WEEKLY TO MAINTAIN COVER CREDIBILITY
KEEP EMERGENCY TELEPORTATION COORDINATES MEMORIZED, NOT WRITTEN
